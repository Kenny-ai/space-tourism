# Orbital explorer texture credits

Downloaded October 27, 2026. Rendered sizes, rotation speeds, lighting, atmosphere, colors, and camera paths are illustrative. No source agency or author endorsement is implied.

## Solar System Scope / INOVE

Earth, clouds, Moon, and Mars are based on NASA imagery/elevation maps distributed by Solar System Scope under **CC BY 4.0**.

Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

- `earth.webp`: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_daymap.jpg
- `clouds.webp`: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_earth_clouds.jpg
- `moon.webp`: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_moon.jpg
- `mars.webp`: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_mars.jpg

Each source was resized from 2048 × 1024 to 1536 × 768 and converted to WebP quality 84. Solar System Scope's originals include color adjustments and inferred terrain in unmapped regions.

## Europa — NASA/JPL/USGS

`europa.webp` is derived from NASA's Voyager global mosaic, distributed in NASA's official 3D Resources repository:
https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/blob/master/Images%20and%20Textures/Jupiter%20-%20Europa/Jupiter%20-%20Europa.jpg

Converted to WebP quality 85 at its original 1440 × 720 resolution. Rendered with a subtle illustrative tint.

## Titan — NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute

`titan.webp` is derived from **Titan Global Map — June 2015 (PIA19658)**:
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/titan-global-map-june-2015/

Source asset:
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/solar/2023/09/p/i/a/PIA19658-1.jpg

The 800 × 444 source was cropped to the map area (left 36, top 40, width 740, height 370), removing the frame/legend, and converted to WebP quality 85. The grayscale near-infrared surface map is rendered with illustrative amber haze and tint; it is not a visible-light photograph.
